About us

A technology team that speaks plainly

9techlabs builds websites, apps and AI assistants, and helps businesses reach customers on social media and get value from their data.

Our approach

Why we work this way

Most businesses do not need more technology. They need the right piece of it, working properly, explained in words they understand.

That is the job we set ourselves. We treat every project like an experiment in a lab: agree what we are trying to prove, build the smallest thing that proves it, measure the result, and improve from there.

It keeps budgets honest and it means you see working results early, not a long wait followed by a big reveal.

Our promises

What you can expect from us

Four commitments we make on every project, whatever its size.

  • Plain language

    We explain our recommendations without jargon, and we tell you when a cheaper or simpler option would do the job.

  • A clear scope and price

    You know what will be delivered, when, and what it costs before work starts. Changes are agreed with you first.

  • Progress you can see

    You get regular working previews, not status reports, so you can steer the project while it is still easy to change.

  • Ownership stays with you

    Code, designs, accounts and data belong to you. We hand over documentation so you are never locked in to us.

Our clients

Who we work with

Startups launching a first product, established businesses replacing old systems, and teams that need extra hands for a specific piece of work.

Some clients come to us for a single website. Others ask us to look after their site, their app, their marketing and their reporting together. Both are welcome.
