How we work

How a project runs

The same four stages, whether we are building a five-page website or a full product.

  1. Understand

    We learn your business, your customers and what success looks like. You get a written scope and a fixed plan.

  2. Design

    You see how it will look and work before we build it, and we change it until it is right.

  3. Build

    We build in short rounds and show you working progress at each one, so there are no surprises at the end.

  4. Launch and support

    We test, launch and stay on to fix, measure and improve. You are never handed the keys and left alone.

Questions

Common questions

How much does a project cost?

Every project is priced on its scope. After a short conversation you receive a written scope, timeline and price, and nothing starts until you approve it.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the work. Your quote includes a timeline, and we build in short rounds so you see working progress throughout instead of waiting for the end.

Do I own the code and designs?

Yes. Code, designs, accounts and data belong to you, and we hand over documentation so another developer could pick the work up.

Can you work with our existing website or systems?

Yes. We often improve or extend what is already there, and we will tell you plainly when rebuilding would cost less than repairing.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. We stay on to fix issues, measure results and make improvements. The level of support is agreed with you in the scope.

Can we start with only one service?

Yes. Many clients begin with a single website or campaign and add other services later, once the first piece is working.

Tell us what you are building.

Send a few lines about what you need and we will reply with questions, ideas and next steps. Or write to hello@9techlabs.com.
