Case studies

What the work looks like

Recent projects, the problems behind them, what we built and what changed as a result.

A website and WhatsApp booking assistant for a dental clinic

  • Dental care
  • Website design
  • AI assistants and chatbots

We built the clinic a new website and a WhatsApp chatbot that answers patients and books their appointments, so enquiries that used to slip away now become visits.

The challenge

Patients were getting in touch to ask about treatments and book a visit, but too many of those conversations ended before an appointment was confirmed. Every dropped enquiry was a patient the clinic never saw.

What we built

  • A new website for the clinic
  • A WhatsApp chatbot that books appointments inside the conversation
  • Instant answers on WhatsApp to the questions patients ask most

The result

  • More appointments booked
  • Enquiries that were previously dropped now turn into bookings
  • Patients get the information they need without waiting for a reply

SQL Server management and real-time sales dashboards for a retailer

  • Retail
  • Data services

We manage the retailer's SQL Server database and built dashboards that show sales as they happen, so the team no longer waits for a report to know how the day is going.

The challenge

The retailer runs its sales on a SQL Server database that needs continuous care, and the team had no live view of what was selling. Decisions had to wait until figures were pulled together afterwards.

What we built

  • Ongoing management of the SQL Server database
  • Dashboards that track sales in real time
  • Sales figures drawn straight from the live database, with no manual exports

The result

  • Sales are visible as they happen
  • One trusted source for sales figures
  • A database that is looked after continuously, not only when something breaks

A furniture retailer's online store, rebuilt for phones

  • Retail
  • Website design
  • Data services

We replaced a slow, hard-to-edit store with a fast one, and added a sales dashboard fed by clean order data.

The challenge

The store took several seconds to load on a phone, most visitors left at checkout, and staff could not change products without calling a developer. Sales figures lived in three spreadsheets that never agreed.

What we built

  • A redesigned store with a three-step checkout
  • A product editor the shop team uses themselves
  • Order data cleaned and moved into one database
  • A daily sales dashboard for the owner

The result

  • Pages load much faster on mid-range phones
  • More visitors now finish checkout
  • Weekly sales reports no longer need to be assembled by hand

A booking app and AI assistant for a group of clinics

  • Healthcare
  • App development
  • AI assistants and chatbots

Patients now book in an app or ask the assistant, which answers routine questions and passes the rest to reception.

The challenge

Reception phones were busy all morning with the same questions about opening hours, prices and appointment changes. Patients who called after hours gave up and booked elsewhere.

What we built

  • An iPhone and Android app for booking and reminders
  • An assistant on the website and app that answers from the clinic's own policies
  • Handover to reception with the conversation attached
  • Clear limits so the assistant never gives medical advice

The result

  • Most bookings are now made without a phone call
  • Routine questions are answered without staff involvement
  • Fewer missed appointments since reminders were introduced

Social media that fills tables for a restaurant group

  • Hospitality
  • Social media marketing
  • Data services

We built a content plan and tracked campaigns across four locations, with one report showing which posts bring bookings.

The challenge

Each location posted on its own with no plan, ad spend was spread thin, and nobody could say whether a campaign had led to a single reservation.

What we built

  • One brand voice and a monthly content calendar
  • Short video and photo content for each location
  • Paid campaigns with tracking through to the booking page
  • A monthly report that ties spend to reservations

The result

  • Reservations can now be traced back to the post or ad that prompted them
  • Ad budget goes to the campaigns that bring bookings
  • A growing local following around each location
