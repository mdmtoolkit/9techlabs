Website design

Your website is where most customers decide whether to get in touch. We design and build sites that load quickly, read clearly on a phone and make the next step obvious.

What is included

  • Company and marketing websites
  • Online stores and booking sites
  • Layouts designed for phones first
  • Search engine basics: structure, speed and metadata
  • A content editor so you can make changes yourself
  • Redesigns and moves from an older site
  • Hosting setup, security updates and maintenance
  • Analytics so you can see what visitors do

A good fit when your current site looks dated, is hard to update, or gets visitors but few enquiries.

App development

We build apps for iPhone, Android and the web. We start with the smallest version that is useful to real people, release it, and grow it from what they do with it.

What is included

  • Scoping workshops and clickable prototypes
  • iPhone and Android apps
  • Web apps, customer portals and admin dashboards
  • Internal tools that replace spreadsheets
  • Connections to payment, CRM and other systems
  • Testing on real devices
  • App Store and Google Play release
  • Updates, monitoring and support after launch

A good fit when you have a product idea to prove, or a manual process that has outgrown email and spreadsheets.

AI assistants and chatbots

We set up assistants that answer from your own documents, products and policies. They take the routine questions, work around the clock and pass the conversation to a person when it needs one.

What is included

  • Customer support chatbots for your website and messaging channels
  • Internal assistants that search your company documents
  • Lead qualification and appointment booking
  • Connections to your CRM, helpdesk and calendar
  • Rules for what the assistant may and may not answer
  • Handover to your team, with the conversation attached
  • Testing against real customer questions before launch
  • Review of conversations and tuning after launch

A good fit when your team answers the same questions every day, or enquiries arrive outside working hours and go cold.

Social media marketing

We plan, create and run your presence on the platforms your customers use. Every campaign has a goal you can measure, and every month you see what it achieved.

What is included

  • Platform and audience strategy
  • A monthly content calendar you approve
  • Post design, short video and copywriting
  • Paid campaigns on Meta, LinkedIn, TikTok and others
  • Replies to comments and messages
  • Profile setup and clean-up
  • Tracking from ad to enquiry
  • A plain monthly report with next steps

A good fit when you post now and then with no plan, or you spend on ads without knowing what they return.

Data services

Most businesses already hold the data they need, spread across spreadsheets and systems that do not talk to each other. We bring it together, clean it and put it where you can use it.

What is included

  • Data cleanup and removal of duplicates
  • Migration between systems without losing history
  • Dashboards for sales, operations and finance
  • Automated reports in place of manual ones
  • Pipelines that keep systems in sync
  • Database design and tuning
  • Website and app analytics setup
  • Preparing your data for AI assistants

A good fit when reports take days to assemble, two systems disagree, or you are changing software and cannot afford to lose records.
